Radio & radar

Listening in on the radios inside cheap modules and gadgets.

RCWL-0516 microwave radar 790

Everything I could find about a cheap Doppler-radar motion sensor module, an alternative to a PIR sensor. The carrier turned out to be about 3.18 GHz, found with a HackRF.

repo discussion

Efergy energy-monitor decoder

Decoding the radio frames sent by Efergy electricity monitors, using a cheap RTL-SDR dongle.

rtl-sdr-efergy efergy-e2

Camera gimbal protocols

Reverse-engineered radio protocols and control code for the DJI RS3 (Bluetooth LE) and Zifon PT5000 (nRF24L01-style 2.4 GHz) camera gimbals.

dji_rs3_control RS3 web app zifon_pt5000

Satellite navigation

GNSS receivers, RTK positioning and the data behind them.

GNSS / RTK positioning 33

Software, scripts and data for real-time and post-processed kinematic satellite navigation, a low-cost RTK survey kit and a Java GNSS library.

GNSS_RTK RTK_survey java_gnss survey video

GPS Selective Availability switch-off

GPS receiver data and a visualisation of the moment Selective Availability was switched off on 2 May 2000, when civilian accuracy improved about tenfold.

repo

Body signals

ECG, PPG and blood-pressure front ends on small microcontrollers.

ECG front ends

Code and PCBs for TI ADS1x9x ECG front-end chips, including an LPC824 + ADS1292R board and LPC1114 firmware.

ADS1x9xECG LPC824_ADS1292R LPC1114 firmware

PPG, SpO₂ and blood pressure

Tools for analysing photoplethysmogram signals, MAX30102 SpO₂ sensor firmware, and an add-on that turns a low-cost blood pressure monitor into an ambulatory one.

PPG_Tools MAX30102 LPC824_BP

Light, heat & sensing

Spectral, thermal and time-of-flight sensors, and how surfaces warm in sunlight.

AS7265x 18-channel spectrometer 12

C library and utility for the AMS AS7265x spectral sensor (410–940 nm), experiment data, and work with the Hamamatsu C12880MA micro-spectrometer.

as7265x experiments C12880MA

Thermal & time-of-flight cameras

Convert raw radiometric FLIR E4/E8 images into temperatures without proprietary software, plus tools for the Terabee 3Dcam 80×60 time-of-flight camera.

flir_tools terabee_3dcam_tools

Radiative heat balance

A physics simulator that explores how surfaces heat up when exposed to the sun or other radiative sources.

simulator repo
